OMNIBRAIN INDUSTRIAL SAFETY KNOWLEDGE BASE

1. GENERAL INDUSTRIAL SAFETY

Industrial safety focuses on preventing injuries, equipment damage, environmental incidents, and operational disruptions.

All personnel must follow approved safety procedures, wear required personal protective equipment (PPE), and report unsafe conditions immediately.

Common workplace hazards include:
- Moving machinery
- Electrical equipment
- Fire and explosion risks
- Chemical exposure
- Slips, trips, and falls
- High temperature surfaces
- Pressure systems
- Confined spaces
- Working at height

2. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT

Required PPE depends on the work environment and hazard.

Common PPE includes:
- Safety helmet
- Safety glasses
- Protective gloves
- Safety shoes
- Hearing protection
- Face shields
- Respiratory protection
- High-visibility clothing

PPE must be inspected before use and replaced when damaged.

3. MACHINE SAFETY

Operators must never bypass machine guards or safety interlocks.

Before maintenance, inspection, or repair, the machine should be isolated from its energy sources using an approved Lockout/Tagout procedure.

Emergency stop controls should remain accessible and functional.

4. ELECTRICAL SAFETY

Only authorized and qualified personnel should work on electrical systems.

Before electrical maintenance:
- Isolate the power source.
- Apply Lockout/Tagout.
- Verify that the equipment is de-energized.
- Use appropriate electrical PPE.
- Follow approved electrical safety procedures.

Damaged cables, exposed conductors, sparks, or unusual electrical smells must be reported immediately.

5. FIRE SAFETY

Fire risks may involve combustible materials, electrical equipment, flammable liquids, gases, or high-temperature processes.

Employees should know:
- Emergency exits
- Fire alarm locations
- Fire extinguisher locations
- Emergency assembly areas

Fire extinguishers should only be used by trained personnel when it is safe to do so.

6. CHEMICAL SAFETY

Chemical containers must be properly labelled and stored according to their hazards.

Safety Data Sheets should be available for hazardous chemicals.

Personnel handling chemicals must use appropriate PPE and follow approved handling and storage procedures.

Chemical spills must be reported immediately and handled according to the site's emergency response procedure.

7. CONFINED SPACE SAFETY

Confined spaces may contain toxic gases, oxygen-deficient atmospheres, or other dangerous conditions.

Entry should only occur under an approved confined-space procedure.

Required controls may include:
- Atmospheric testing
- Ventilation
- Permit authorization
- Communication
- Rescue planning
- Appropriate PPE

8. WORKING AT HEIGHT

Personnel working at height must use approved fall-protection systems where required.

Ladders, scaffolds, platforms, and harnesses must be inspected before use.

Never use damaged fall-protection equipment.

9. INCIDENT REPORTING

All accidents, near misses, unsafe conditions, and equipment-related incidents should be reported promptly.

Incident information should include:
- Date and time
- Location
- People involved
- Description of the event
- Equipment involved
- Immediate actions taken
- Possible contributing factors

10. EMERGENCY RESPONSE

During an emergency, personnel should remain calm and follow the site's emergency response plan.

If an alarm is activated:
- Stop work when safe.
- Follow evacuation instructions.
- Move to the designated assembly point.
- Do not re-enter until authorized.

11. PREDICTIVE MAINTENANCE

Industrial equipment can be monitored using parameters such as:
- Temperature
- Vibration
- Pressure
- Current
- Voltage
- Noise
- Flow rate

Abnormal trends may indicate developing equipment problems.

Predictive maintenance can help identify potential failures before they cause unplanned downtime.

12. MACHINE ANOMALIES

Examples of abnormal machine behavior include:
- Excessive vibration
- Unusual temperature increase
- Unexpected noise
- Pressure fluctuations
- Increased energy consumption
- Repeated alarms
- Unexpected shutdowns

An anomaly should be investigated using approved operational and maintenance procedures.

13. SAFETY PRINCIPLE

OmniBrain should provide safety information as decision-support guidance.

For serious or emergency situations, users must follow their organization's approved emergency procedures and contact qualified safety or emergency personnel.

OmniBrain must not claim that an AI recommendation replaces professional safety judgment, site procedures, regulations, or authorized personnel.
